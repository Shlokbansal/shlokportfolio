Engineering problem

Pressure characterization required a consistent measurement approach and a documented procedure. Sensor placement, loading conditions, and worst-case use needed to be considered before comparing measurements.

My contribution

As V&V lead, I developed a FlexiForce sensing SOP and supported controlled verification testing. I worked on worst-case test considerations and verification documentation for the women’s-health device project.

Technical approach

  • Define the measurement method

    Documented the FlexiForce sensing procedure, including how to set up and use the measurement approach consistently.

  • Control the experiment

    Structured verification testing around controlled conditions and worst-case considerations.

  • Make the evidence traceable

    Prepared verification documentation to connect the test procedure and its observations to the device-development work.

Result

Sensor-based V&V

controlled characterization and test procedures

Produced a documented sensing procedure and supported controlled verification testing and regulatory-submission preparation.

No proprietary pressure values, device geometry, or regulatory clearance claims are presented.

Lessons learned

A sensor reading needs context. Clear setup instructions and controlled loading conditions are essential for making results repeatable and useful to the next person reviewing the evidence.
